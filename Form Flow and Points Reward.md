## **The Earning Flow (Passive & Active)**

1. **Home Screen:** Features a small "Reward Progress" widget or a floating coin icon showing current balance (e.g., "500 pts").  
2. **Video Player:** A subtle "Watch & Earn" progress bar appears after 10 minutes of continuous play.  
3. **Post-Watch Feedback:** A "Rate this Movie" form appears.  
   * *Input:* Star rating (1–5) and optional text review.  
   * *Reward:* "Submit for \+20 Points."

## **The Points & Rewards Hub**

4. **Points Dashboard:** \* **Header:** Total Balance (Large font).  
   * **Earning History:** A list of recent activities (e.g., "Daily Login: \+10," "Watched 'The Creator': \+50").  
   * **Quests/Tasks:** "Watch 3 episodes today," "Refer a friend," or "Complete your profile."  
5. **Rewards Marketplace:** \* **Categories:** Subscription Discounts, Rental Coupons, Ad-free hours, or Physical Merchandise.  
   * **Visuals:** Cards showing the "cost" (e.g., "1-Month Premium — 2,000 pts").

## **Redemption Form Flow**

6. **Reward Detail Page:** Explains the terms and conditions of the specific reward.  
   * *Action:* "Redeem Now" button.  
7. **Confirmation Modal (The "Form"):**  
   * *Verification:* "Are you sure you want to spend 500 points on a 24-hour Rental Voucher?"  
   * *Dropdown/Toggle:* Option to "Apply now" or "Save for later."  
8. **Success Screen:** Confirms the points have been deducted and provides a "Use Reward" shortcut.
